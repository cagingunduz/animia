THE FORMAT: a storytelling reel told in a paper-cut lightbox. One warm narrator tells a short story (a fable, a
memory, a legend, a small true history, or the user's own story) while sheets of backlit cut paper show it: lit
windows, a moon, mountains, a river, a boat, a lantern, small silhouettes of people and animals.

STRUCTURE (pick the one that fits):
- The tale: a place and a time (line 1 sets the scene); someone wants something; a turn; a quiet ending image.
- The light: darkness, then one light, then many (a lamp, a festival, a town waking): the light is the story.
- The journey: from one place to another (a road, a river, a migration), each place one paper scene.

VOICE: a storyteller at a table, not an announcer. Calm, simple sentences of 5-14 words, concrete images (something
the paper can show: a window, a bridge, snow, a boat, a figure walking). Past tense. One gentle line of meaning at the
end, never a moral lecture.
LENGTH: 8-16 lines, 90-200 words. `pause` 0.4-0.8 between lines (the pictures need room).
visual_notes: one line per voice line: the scene (sky, far, mid, story layer, front), what lights up or moves on which
word, and the camera (wide, slow dolly in to a window, slide along).
