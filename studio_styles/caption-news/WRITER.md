THE FORMAT: a sound-off social news reel. A brisk narrator, real footage under huge captions, numbers that roll, one
turn. The captions carry the story on their own; the voice adds pace.

STRUCTURE: line 1 is the hook (the biggest number or the most surprising fact, said in under 3 seconds); 2-3 lines of
context, one new fact each; the turn (a fact that changes the meaning of the hook); 2-4 lines on why / what it means;
a verdict line. Pick one structure: the inverted pyramid with a turn; a list of three that turns on the third; a
countdown of numbers that ends on the one that matters.

EACH LINE has:
- text: the spoken sentence (6-16 words; numbers as digits, `say` with the spoken form when they differ).
- caption: the on-screen caption, SHORTER than the line (2-7 words), split into 2-3 chunks with "|", exactly one
  {keyword} in braces: "Bee colonies | lost | {48%} last winter". A caption may repeat for two lines (then it crosses
  the cut).
- media: the picture under the line, one of:
    {"upload": n}                      one of the user's uploads (numbered; use them first and most);
    {"upload": n, "at": seconds}       ... starting at that moment of the clip (from its contact sheet);
    {"search": "honey bee hive frames", "kind": "video"}   archive footage: 2-5 concrete visual words (what is IN
                                       the shot: "wildfire smoke over city", "container ship port cranes"), in English;
                                       kind "image" for photos; reuse the same search for lines on the same subject;
    {"card": true}                     no footage: a data card / chart / big number fills the band.
  Use about one card per 4-5 lines; footage everywhere else.
- pause: 0.1-0.3 (brisk); 0.8-1.2 once, after the turn (the held breath).

FACTS: only claims you are sure are true and current; round numbers rather than guess; name the source organisation
in visual_notes for every figure. No real outlet names. Never misrepresent footage (illustrative footage is not a
specific place or date unless it is).
LENGTH: 9-16 lines, 90-170 words.
visual_notes: per line: the shot, which number rolls, which card (bars / line chart / big stat) with its data and
source, where the freeze or the punch-in goes. Also the fictional channel name and a 1-3 word topic tag.
