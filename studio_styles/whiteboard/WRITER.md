THE FORMAT: a whiteboard explainer reel. One narrator explains one idea ("how does X work", "why does Y happen") while
it is drawn live on a board. The viewer learns from the drawings: every line must name something that can be drawn
(a diagram, an arrow, a number, a labelled thing) or a word that will be written.

STRUCTURE (pick the one that fits the topic best):
- The question: line 1 asks it plainly ("Why is the sky blue?"); then 3-5 steps, each one a drawing that builds on
  the last; a twist or a surprising number; the answer in one sentence, circled.
- The process: a pipeline drawn left to right / top to bottom, one stage per line, and what goes wrong if one fails.
- The comparison: two things drawn side by side by two pens, until they meet in one conclusion.

VOICE: calm, clear, a little warm; British narrator by default. Sentences of 6-16 words. Say numbers the way they are
spoken in `say` when the caption form differs ("1.5 km" / "one and a half kilometres").
LENGTH: 10-18 lines, 110-220 words. Only true claims; drop anything you are not sure of.
