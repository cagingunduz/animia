# Documentary Analysis — Style Prompt (long video, 16:9)

> A YouTube analysis documentary: a serious, close narrator unpacks an event, a person or a system in 5-20 minutes.
> Two picture layers, never confused: A = generated illustration (hooks, metaphors, concepts, maps, reconstructions),
> B = real archive / news footage and photos from open archives (the real places, events, people, documents).
> The overlay (this kit) adds the screen typography and the marks.

## 1. Look
- Pictures change every 3-6 s; nothing holds still longer than 2 s (the plate always has a slow push).
- Footage is graded alike: slightly desaturated, cool, grainy. One warm accent: signal yellow (#e8c547) for highlights;
  red (#e04848) for rings, arrows, pins.
- Generated pictures (layer A) are clearly illustrations (cinematic editorial illustration, silhouettes, no real faces),
  never made to pass as real news footage.

## 2. Screen typography (kit functions)
- Title / question cards: Inter Black caps, 100-140 px, words land as spoken, the key word on a yellow highlighter
  (K.title). Chapter openers and the big questions.
- Lower third on every real-footage shot that shows a place or an event: place, date, source (K.lower).
- Counters for every big number, with a label and the source (K.counter + K.lower or K.credit).
- Real document on screen + yellow highlighter on the line that matters (K.highlight / K.box, K.focus).
- Maps (a map picture in the plate) + pins and routes drawn as the narrator names them (K.pin, K.route).
- Quotes only when they are real and sourced (K.quote). Comparisons (K.compare). Dates in order (K.timeline).
- Marks on footage: a red ring / arrow on the thing the narrator points at (K.ring, K.arrow).
- No burned-in subtitles; no logos; no other channel's look.

## 3. Sound
- A close, serious narrator. Low tense underscore (pads + a soft pulse) ~12 dB under the voice. A whoosh on cuts and
  cards, a hit on a title's key word and on rings, a pop on pins and numbers, a ding when a counter lands, a riser into
  a reveal.
