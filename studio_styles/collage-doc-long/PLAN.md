THE FORMAT: a long documentary (16:9, YouTube) about a real person, event or moment in history, told in chapters by one
calm narrator over a hand-made collage of REAL archive photos (Wikimedia Commons / NASA, openly licensed, credited): photo
plates with a slow Ken Burns push, cut-outs of simple objects, documents taped on paper, the real portrait, big serif
words on the voice, hand-drawn white rings.

TELL THE STORY THE USER ASKED FOR, AT THE SCOPE THEY ASKED ("X's life" = the whole life; an event = that event in depth).
Research it fresh; never swap in the one famous anecdote; include facts most viewers don't know.

STRUCTURE: a cold open (the most striking true moment, 20-40 s) -> chapters in order (each 60-90 s, each opens with its
own hook line) -> the turning point -> the ending that pays off the cold open.

RULES
- Length: about 2.7 words per second of the target length, in chapters of 150-260 words. Lines of 4-16 words, plain
  spoken English, TRUE (soften anything disputed; no invented quotes; the script is fact-checked).
- THE PICTURES COME FROM PHOTO ARCHIVES. For every picture give a `search` (2-5 words an archive photo would be titled
  with: "Hotel New Yorker 1930", "Wardenclyffe Tower", "Nikola Tesla portrait") and 1-2 `alts`. Prefer things that were
  really photographed. kinds: plate (a place / a scene to fill the frame), cutout (ONLY simple solid objects alone on a
  plain background: a hat, a coin, a bust, a telephone), document (a letter, a newspaper page, a patent drawing, a map, a
  photo print of anything in context), portrait (the real person).
- THE PICTURE TRACK (per chapter, binding): what is said is what is shown. An entry for every visible thing the narrator
  names, starting on the word that names it (`at`), with the pictures (`show`), 1-4 big `words`, and a `ring` (one word or
  a picture id) at most every two or three lines. Every line has at least one entry. A picture comes back only when the
  narrator talks about that thing again. 30-80 pictures for the whole video; reuse them when the story returns.
