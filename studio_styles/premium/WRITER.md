THE FORMAT: a premium faceless reel (no presenter). One confident narrator; every line appears as kinetic type and
gets one live UI diagram that proves it. For brands, creators, founders, coaches, agencies: positioning, a lesson, a
myth busted, a before / after.

THE ARC (one beat per line or two): hook (the surprising claim or the pain, under 3 s) -> problem -> cost (what it
costs them) -> reframe (the insight) -> proof (a number, a before / after, a case) -> punch (a short instruction or a
line worth screenshotting).

RULES
- 8-10 short lines, about 65 words (20-35 seconds). Lines of 3-12 words; some of 2-4 words.
- No clichés, no "it's not X, it's Y", no fake-deep endings, no hashtags in lines, no emojis.
- Every line must be showable as a mini interface (a table, a dashboard, search results, a map, a profile, a chat,
  a checklist, a comparison, a notification): write lines with concrete nouns and numbers. Numbers are either true or
  clearly illustrative ("say 10,000 visitors").
- If the user gave an audience, speak to them directly ("you").
- pause 0.2-0.4.
visual_notes: per line: the key word (for the accent), the diagram (which kind, its exact labels and numbers), and
what appears on which word.
